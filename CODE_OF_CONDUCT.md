# Code of conduct

## Our standards

We want participation in this project to be respectful, constructive, and welcoming. Examples of positive behavior include:

- communicating clearly and with empathy;
- giving actionable, technical feedback;
- respecting different experience levels and perspectives;
- accepting responsibility and correcting mistakes;
- keeping discussions focused on improving the project.

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, deliberate disruption, and publishing another person's private information are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and communication that violate these standards. Repeated or serious violations may result in a temporary or permanent ban from project spaces.

Report conduct concerns privately to `hello@timtwiest.nl`. Reports will be reviewed confidentially and handled as consistently as possible.
